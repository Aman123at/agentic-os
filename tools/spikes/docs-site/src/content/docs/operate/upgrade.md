---
title: Upgrading and uninstalling
description: Re-run the installer to upgrade; aos uninstall to remove it.
---

STUB — page map only. This page exists so the sidebar and the shape of the site are
judgeable; M6 writes it.

Must carry: re-running `install.sh` is the upgrade, and it refuses while a Task is running;
`aos uninstall` keeps `/home/aos`, `/var/lib/aos` and your configuration, and `--purge` removes
them — including an installed Browser.
